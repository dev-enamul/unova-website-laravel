We are a team of builders, designers, and AI specialists based in Dhaka. Our mission is simple — help every business in Bangladesh stop wasting time on manual work and start using AI to grow faster.

Our Story

Born in Bangladesh. Built for Business.

Unova started with one observation: most businesses in Bangladesh still run on spreadsheets, phone calls, and WhatsApp groups. It's slow, messy, and costs time and money every single day.

We decided to fix that. We started with real estate — one of the most process-heavy industries in Bangladesh — and built UnovaEstate, a complete AI-powered business management system for real estate companies. The results spoke for themselves.

Now we're expanding our Nova product suite across 20+ industries with one clear goal: help every business in Bangladesh automate their work using AI — so they can spend less time on admin and more time on growth.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These are the principles we live by — in every product we build, every client we serve, and every problem we solve.

AI First Thinking

We don't add AI as an afterthought. Every product and solution we build starts with the question: how can AI make this faster, smarter, and more useful?

Reliability Over Flash

We build software that works quietly and consistently. We don't care how impressive a demo looks — we care that it works perfectly every single day.

Built for Real Businesses

We build for real business owners in Bangladesh — not for tech experts. Everything we make is simple to use, even if the technology behind it is complex.

Honest Partnerships

We tell you what you need, not just what you ask for. We believe in clear pricing, honest advice, and long-term relationships built on real results.

Results Over Process

We measure success by the impact we create for your business — saved hours, faster workflows, and more revenue. Not just by delivering a product.

Move Fast, Build Right
